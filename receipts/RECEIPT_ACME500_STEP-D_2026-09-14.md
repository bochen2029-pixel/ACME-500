## D0 · rows on change; the TICK-driven loop

**What changed.** A person's hold (reasons 1 blocked and 2 no attention) is written when the hold in force changes — the seat or the reason — and not per seat per day; any touch of the cell (a fetch, an act, a mid-case hold, a handoff, a decision, an effect) ends the hold in force, so the next hold is a change and a row. The resident's hold likewise on a change of (reason, band), ended by an effect or an undo. A `STRATUM` row is written when the draw in force changes (kind, canary rate or audit rate) and stands until then. The hold in force and the draw in force are folded fields (`Obligation::hold_reason/hold_seat/mhold_reason/mhold_band`; the ledger's per-cell stratum kind and both rates), compared by O14. The meters count what they counted before (`holds recorded`, `held (a row, with its margin)` are per cell-day, row or not), so no reading moves. Reason-3 holds, a day that ends mid-case, carry state and stay per occurrence.

**The loop is the world port's.** Intake lands (`ARRIVE` rows), a `TICK` is folded (`tick_fold`: the row is written and the ledger's day moves with it), the governor draws, the resident runs its period **on the day the ledger says** — `Resident::period` and `grade` no longer take a day; the governor's `draw_strata` reads the ledger's — then the incumbent works, the world settles, the governor folds the outcomes and steps the ladder. The `TICK` row's value is wall seconds since the last tick, written 0 by the sim and never read by the machine; the dynamic O25 (D3) varies it.

## D1 · the durable tape, the checkpoint, `--dump`; O18

**The file** (`include/acme/tapefile.h`, kernel-side, scanned by O25a). A tape is a directory of segments, one million records each, every record the 40 bytes `Rec` occupies in memory (packed; `static_assert 40`), so the wire format is the struct and `memcmp` stays the theorem. A segment opens with a packed 64-byte header — magic, record version, the alphabet pin, its index, the row index of its first record, and the chain head at its start — so a reader recovers the head by re-walking one segment and checks each segment's header against the running head. The tape sees every record the moment it is chained (`Tape::sink`). A torn tail (a last segment whose length is not header + k × 40) is truncated to the last whole record and the bytes are reported; complete bytes whose head does not match the next header are corruption and the open refuses. The sidecar `tape.meta.json` carries the pins, the judge hashes, the rows, the head and the cursor, rewritten by atomic rename.

**The checkpoint** (`include/acme/checkpoint.h`, main-side). At the close of every tenth live period (`--ckpt-every`), `ckpt/NNNNNN.state` holds everything a future row depends on that the seed does not regenerate: the ledger (cells, index, open set, day, memo, strata, meter), the incumbent's meter, the seats' live state and the writ, the resident (field with its Adam state, hand, stats, supervision, memo hashes, the compiled table), the governor (salt, ladder, rates), the two judges' learned competence, the replay's counts, and the tape's cursor and head; `NNNNNN.meta` — day, cursor, chain head, the BLAKE2b of the state file — is written **last, by rename**. A state without its meta was in flight when the process died and a restore skips it.

**The restore** (`--resume`). Load the segments and re-walk the chain; take the newest checkpoint whose meta exists, whose state digests to what the meta says and whose head is the tape's head at its cursor; **cut the tape back to the cursor** in memory and on disk — the rows of the periods in flight were uncommitted (in a real lane they come back from the source's own cursor; in the sim the plant regenerates them) — put a `NOTE` row on the tape (code 1; the rows and the torn bytes dropped; `RF_TORN_OR_SURVEY`), and run the world port's loop from the day after. A `NOTE` is a row of no period: the next `TICK` does not count it, which is what makes the continued tape equal the unkilled one row for row.

## D2 · the switch, the pill, the gate as a hashed library; O19, O30, O2's pressure axis

**The gate is its own header** (`include/acme/gate.h`: the verdicts, the reasons, the alphabet pin, `GateIn`/`gate()`, the packing of the gate's inputs; it includes `core.h` and `firm.h` only). `tools/gate_hash.py` pins its BLAKE2b-256 in `include/acme/gate_hash.inc`, the binary embeds the pin and prints it in the automate's header and in `tape.meta.json`, and **O30a** (a tool oracle like O28) refuses a tree whose `gate.h` no longer digests to its pin; its lie changes one token of a scratch copy (`rung <= 0` → `rung < 0`) and requires the mismatch. An intended change to the gate is `--write` plus a line in the receipt. The pin at D2: `d1115a674638eba9`.

**The switch.** `Switch {off, shadow, live, stop}` (`gate.h`). The sim starts on `--switch`; a real port reads `intellect.state` (`--switch-file PATH`) every period and, on a change, writes a `NOTE` row (code 2, b = the new switch) that the ledger folds — the machine never writes the file and never reads it; it reads `Ledger::sw`, which is a fold of the `HEADER` row and the notes. The gate takes the switch as an input like any other: `off` is the first check and a hold with the new reason `switch-off` (the alphabet pin moves: `0x76223b0c` → `0x2dc5517b`, named). `shadow`: the verdicts are as at `live`, the hand writes the `EFFECT` (or the resident's `ESCALATE`) with `RF_SHADOW` and mutates nothing, the incumbent proceeds; the fold gives a shadow row no effect. `stop`: the period returns before it reads. A shadow effect is a row on change, like a hold since D0: the cell carries the shadow verdict in force (`Obligation::shadow_key`, a fold field compared by O14) and the hand writes again only when via, choice or band changes — the first shadow run at 500 seats wrote 7,112,133 `EFFECT` rows before that rule and 124,190 after.

**The pill.** `intellect.heartbeat.json`, written by the port by rename at the close of every period (`--pill PATH`, default beside the tape): the day, the rows, the chain head, the switch, the judge, the wall clock. `tools/pill.py` reads it and prints ALIVE or STALLED past N periods (default three). The machine cannot write it.

**The gate's inputs travel on the row.** For every `EFFECT` to be re-derivable from rows alone, the two gate inputs no row carried — the field's sharpness and the conformal novelty — are packed into the `EFFECT` row's `b` beside the previous state (12 bits each; `pack_gate_inputs`). `UNDO` carries its own previous state; the fold does not read `b` on an `EFFECT`; the observer's contract is untouched (`value` stays the class value).
