=== THE NUMBER A REAL PROGRAMME CANNOT GET ============================
  true effect, paired arms            -4.36 points of good-outcome rate (total cost delta $-589.8M)
  within-firm canary estimate        +27.37  (unattended acts 12122 vs human-decided 78871 in the same firm)
  assisted, not in the estimate     95963 drafts and warrants a person executed, 26.0% good
  BIAS OF THE ESTIMATOR              +31.73

  The canary is not measuring the same thing as the twin, and the gap is not
  noise. Two mechanisms drive it and both grow with the canary rate: the machine
  is routed the cases it was licensed for, which is selection; and the humans in
  the same firm are working a queue the machine already drained, which is
  interference. So r has an interior optimum, not just the floor n0/V that the
  identifiability arithmetic gives. Raise r to clear n0 faster and you contaminate
  the comparison; lower it to protect the comparison and you never clear n0.

  Budget for a bias of about this size in any real programme. It is the term the
  three single-way documents all left out, and a simulation is the only place it
  can be measured at all.
