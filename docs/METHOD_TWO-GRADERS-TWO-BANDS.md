# Two graders, two bands, all clocks at once

*2026-09-13 · Claude Opus 5 · the polished single way to automate as much of an organization as possible, made from two answers that disagreed on one point: `THE-SINGLE-WAY_REPLAY-THE-FIRM-BEFORE-YOU-RUN-IT` (replay is the fast grader) and `THE-SINGLE-WAY_THE-PORTFOLIO-OF-WAGERS` (only intervention licenses; start every class at once). Each was half right about the same thing. The half each had is the half the other lacked, and the join is sharper than either.*

## The one disagreement, resolved

The replay document said history is the fast grader and licenses in hours. The portfolio document said history cannot license at all, because a logged tape carries outcomes only for what the incumbent chose, and the region where a license would extend authority is the region where the machine would have chosen differently, which no recording contains. The selective-labels point is correct. The conclusion drawn from it is too strong, and the replay document's four-cell table already showed why.

There are two graders and they have disjoint support.

**History** grades with zero latency and restricted support. Where the machine's choice on a settled instance is the choice the firm made, the action is identical and its outcome is on the tape, so the machine's decision is graded by the world, not by agreement. Agreement only names the band; the arrival grades it. On the other side of the table, where the machine would have chosen differently and the firm's choice worked, there is no label, and nothing on any tape will ever supply one.

**The world** grades with full support and a latency of one term. The only way to learn what happens when the machine chooses differently is to let it choose, on a fraction of live instances it cannot predict, and wait.

The unit of history's license is therefore the band, not the class: the region of the input space, observable through the margin, where the machine and the incumbent coincide. FACTOR already licenses per class, band and verb; this is the reason it had to. The unit of the world's license is the class-band that history could not reach, and that is the wager.

Two consequences follow, and they are the whole method.

First, the compile step is what makes history's band large. A solver compiled from the firm's own applications and fitted to the firm's own boundary log reproduces the firm's behavior on the mass. So the agreement band is most of the decision mass by construction, and it is licensed in hours. That is the headcount case: the seat leaves and the decision quality stays. The disagreement band is where the trunk's prior and the residual differ from the firm, which is the firm's alpha or its defect, and that band licenses at the world's clock. That is the better-than-human case, and it is smaller.

Second, even the agreement band needs a live canary, at a low rate, for a different reason: history graded the decision, not the machine's execution of it. Predicting is easier than running, everywhere. The canary in the agreement band grades execution; the canary in the disagreement band grades the decision. Both run from day one.

## The single solution

An organization automates at the rate the world grades machine-made decisions and no faster, so the method is to make the world grade as much as possible, as early as possible, using both graders on their own support. Compile every decision class at once from the firm's applications and boundary log. Replay the settled history on a resident to license, per class and band, the region where the machine's choice was the firm's, graded by outcomes that have already landed; prune what fails; set each class's ceiling by coverage. From the first day, canary every admitted class on a stratum the machine cannot predict, at the rate the identifiability floor sets, under one envelope, from one resident process beside the store, with the signer executing the machine's choice verbatim where the law reserves a signature. Let licenses climb per band on arrivals and on nothing else. Compile stable classes into routines with no model. Delete the interior where the decide fraction reads zero, and let the functions that scaled with headcount leave by arithmetic once the external work is licensed. The residual is warrant, counterparty, frame and thin tape, and only the last two move.

## The method, in order, with the number each step prints

0. **Instrument the boundary.** Change data capture off the systems of record into one append-only, hash-chained tape: events in, effects out, outcomes when they land, timestamps, 64-bit revisions. Never the interior. Prints: events, effects, the fraction of effects with a landed outcome.
1. **The schema map.** Ten lines per wire, written by a person. The only authored object besides the writ.
2. **Compile every class at once, from the application.** The frontier reads what the firm already paid people to write in executable form: the query log by screen is the join graph, the validation rules are the invariants, the screen layout is a saliency prior, the write-back is the label; the regulator's policy where one exists. It clusters the tape into classes, writes a context spec per class, proposes invariants, and projects class geometry from the trunk. Prints: class count, and the rank probe's saturation.
3. **Verify invariants and fit the residual.** Zero violations over history becomes a mask before normalization; the rest are soft terms. Fit the firm as a low-rank deviation from the trunk's prior on cached activations, with leave-one-out error in closed form and field ablation as a batched downdate. Prints: invariants held over proposed; residual norm per class; coverage per class; the load-bearing field set per class.
4. **Replay the settled history.** The resident re-decides every settled period, every open cell at once, under the masks, through the gate. Per class and margin band: agreement rate, outcome-graded accuracy on the agreement band, the firm's own error rate, the decide fraction. Prints: the bands history can license, the classes to prune or merge, the coverage ceilings.
5. **License the agreement bands.** One e-process per class and band, paired against the firm's arm on the same instances. Bands where the machine's coinciding choice beats the firm's baseline, with both sides of the threshold above the sample floor, are licensed for unattended action. Prints: licensed fraction of decision mass from history, which is most of it if the compile was honest.
6. **Canary everything, from day one, at the floor.** A fraction r_c ≥ n₀ / V_c of each admitted class's live instances, drawn by keyed hash with a salt held in the immutable floor, is decided by the machine and executed: reversible effects through the integrator with the inverse recorded first; irreversible effects through the warrant terminal, the signer executing the machine's choice verbatim, a refusal logged as a veto row that grades nothing. In the agreement band the canary grades execution; in the disagreement band it grades the decision. Prints: machine-chosen outcomes received this week, per class and band, against n₀. That is the counter on the wall.
7. **Climb on arrivals only.** Agreement, the twin's rollouts, leave-one-out error and a green battery admit; none license. The e-detector narrows on evidence with no signature; kappa demotes a class whose license costs more attention than it saves. Prints: rung per class and band; kappa forecast, then measured.
8. **Compile out.** A band whose decisions are near-deterministic over N instances becomes a routine, replay-tested bit-identically, with the judged path kept under it as control. Prints: the tier histogram, with the model's share falling and the frontier bill with it.
9. **Delete the interior by arithmetic.** A class whose decide fraction reads zero is a round trip with nothing in it and it goes. The cost centers whose headcount tracked headcount rather than external volume leave by N = E / (1 − α) once the E-work is licensed, in that order, because deleting them first breaks the people still doing the E-work. Prints: E after compression, and N.

## The arithmetic

| quantity | rule | where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| the canary rate | r_c ≥ n₀ / V_c per term; arithmetic at the floor, a writ cost above it | the identifiability floor |
| the calendar | T_org ≈ max over wager bands of rungs × n₀ / (r_c · V_c), if every clock starts on day one; the sum if run as serial pilots | the world's clock |
| the signatures | one per class per rung, a few hundred over the program; never one per decision | the serial ratifier |
| the automation | Σ over classes of mass × min(coverage, licensed band mass) | the coverage ceiling |
| the headcount | N = E / (1 − α), with α-functions leaving after E is licensed | the cascade |
| the model bill | compile once per class; extract per event on the resident; solve one launch per period; counsel on thin margins only | four jobs at four clocks |

A sketch, to be replaced by receipts: a firm at 10⁶ decision events a month across 300 classes, Zipf-distributed, with 25 classes carrying 80 percent of the mass. History licenses the agreement band of the head in the first week. Head classes at 2,000 instances a quarter need r ≈ 2.5 percent for n₀ = 50 on the disagreement band; four rungs at a term each licenses that band in about a year, every class's clock running in parallel. Tail classes at 40 a quarter cannot clear n₀ inside a term at any rate and are merged, given a two-term license, or printed as human. Signatures over the year: a few hundred.

## The first week, on one wire

Pick the wire whose world answers fastest and whose invariants are already written down. Capture change data into the tape. Compile the top twenty classes by mass from the application. Replay the last settled quarter: zero invariant violations, coverage and agreement per band, the prune, the agreement bands licensed. Run the α/E regression on payroll against the order book. Turn on the canaries at the floor rate for every admitted class, reversible ones through the integrator, irreversible ones through the signer executing verbatim. Put one counter on the wall: machine-chosen outcomes received this week, per class and band, against n₀. Everything else in the program is upstream of that counter or downstream of it.

## The kills

- If the agreement band licensed from history fails its execution canary at a rate above the replay's stated error, history was grading a decision the machine cannot run, and the band drops to rung 1 until the canary clears.
- If the disagreement-band canaries, run at the floor rate for a term, produce outcome rates no better than the incumbent's on the same stratum, the compile is wrong or the class is two classes.
- If the trunk-initialized geometry does not beat random geometry on held-out next-event likelihood, the compile step is doing all the work and the solver should be dumber.
- If the α/E regression finds no cost center that tracks headcount, the cascade is a story and the middle must be earned class by class.
- If a class licensed on arrivals is demoted by kappa within a term, the ladder's bars were wrong.
- If replay coverage and canary outcomes disagree on a head class by more than the replay's stated error, the replay loses its admitting role too.

## What each document got wrong, so the record shows it

The replay document said every class earns its license in hours. Only the band where the machine's choice was the firm's does; the rest waits for the wager. The portfolio document said replay earns no license and is a certificate the actor wrote. The label on a replay is the world's, and on the coinciding band it is a full grade. The portfolio document also let the twin and the replay share one sentence; they are different objects, and only one of them may promote.

## The line

There are two graders: history, with no latency and support only where the machine agrees with the past, and the world, with full support and a term of latency. Compile so that the first grader's support covers the mass, license that in hours, wager the rest on every class at once at the floor rate under one envelope, climb on arrivals only, and the middle leaves by arithmetic. What remains was never a capability problem.
