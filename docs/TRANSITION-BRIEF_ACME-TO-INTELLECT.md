### The design changes that make the simulator and the product the same program, so that the only thing that ever changes is which world stands behind the port and what the switch file says

The operator's ambition, restated: ACME-500 grows from sim to digital twin to headless trainer to the full suite and then **becomes** the software that runs a real organization — INTELLECT-500 — with nothing changing but a mode and a name. ## §0 · The answer in one breath

The simulator and the product are the same program **if and only if the machine touches the world through exactly one port — rows in, effects out, outcomes back — and the world behind that port is swappable.** ACME-500 already has the machine, the gate, the ladder, the canary, kappa, the tape and the battery; what it does not yet have is the port. Today the resident reads `World` and `Firm` structs directly, computes its own completeness from planted determinant weights, folds planted `decide_frac` into its feature vector and its kappa denominator, holds the arena's salt in its own `Writ`, and depends on fields (`via`, `day_due`, `dep`) that never reach the tape. None of that is wrong for a falsifier; all of it is what stops the falsifier from becoming the controller. The redesign is one cut and four worlds: split `World` into a **Plant** (planted truth; sim only) and a **Ledger** (a fold of the tape; both modes); make every machine input a row; put the salt, the license table and the switch in files the machine reads and never writes; and give the port four implementations — **synthetic** (planted), **fitted** (the twin of a real firm), **replay** (a settled tape), **real** (CDC in, valve out). Then a mode is a pair, (world, switch), ACME is (synthetic, live), INTELLECT is (real, off → shadow → live), the tape is the same 36-byte record with two fields added, the observer draws either, and the oracle battery runs in production with its lies planted on the shadow stratum. The name is a header field.

## §2 · The mode matrix

Two axes. Nothing else varies.

| | **switch = off** (watch, judge, record; nothing leaves) | **shadow** (verdicts written, effects drafted, none executed) | **live** (effects through the valve at the licensed rate) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **world = synthetic** (the Plant; planted truth; the battery's home) | the incumbent sim (`--sim`) | the machine's replay on the sim's own history | ACME-500 as today (`--automate`, `--twin`, `--multiverse`) |
| **world = fitted** (the Plant with parameters estimated from a real tape — the twin of one firm) | — | forecast: FORESEE over the fitted firm, prune only | the gym: many fitted-and-randomized firms per card; trains the value head and the driver; **never licenses** |
| **world = replay** (a settled real tape, re-decided) | the four-cell table; licenses the agreement band | — | — |
| **world = real** (CDC in, valve out, outcomes from counterparties) | **INTELLECT-500 day one**: the resident on the stream, inert | INTELLECT in shadow: drafts diffed against what people do | INTELLECT live: canaries at r ≥ n₀/V, climbing on arrivals |

ACME is the first row. INTELLECT is the last. The diagonal from top-left to bottom-right is the operator's arc — sim → twin → trainer → product — and every cell is the same binary. The verdict law says what each cell may mint: synthetic and fitted worlds **prune** and rank; replay **licenses the agreement band**; only the real world licenses the disagreement band, one term late, at the identifiability floor.

## §3 · The invariant that makes one program of it

State it as a signature, because a signature can be checked by a build:

> **`period(ledger = fold(tape ≤ h), clock) → { verdict rows, effect requests }`**, where `fold` is deterministic and integer-accumulated, the ledger contains nothing that is not derivable from rows, the effect requests go to a port that returns nothing but future rows, and the machine's translation unit does not link the plant.

Under that signature the four worlds differ only in who appends `ARRIVE`, `CONTEXT`, `DECIDE` (human), `OUTCOME` rows and who consumes effect requests. The synthetic Plant appends them from planted truth; the fitted Plant from estimated parameters; replay appends them from a file at machine speed; the real world appends them from CDC adapters at the world's pace and consumes effects through the valve. **The machine cannot tell which world it is in, and that is the property.** It is also fusord's property already: the kernel tails a spool and writes a tape, and nothing in it knows whether the spool is a desk or a test fixture.

Three consequences drive every seam below: (1) every field the gate, the ladder, the field or the compile step reads must be a row or a fold of rows; (2) planted truth must be unlinkable from the machine, not merely unread; (3) the clock, the salt, the license table and the switch must be inputs the machine cannot write.

## §4 · The seams, one by one

Each seam: what the code does today (line), the change, the INTELLECT-mode implementation, the invariant, and the oracle that would catch a violation. The oracle names continue the battery (O14…).

**S1 · The world port: split `World` into `Plant` and `Ledger`.**
Today `World` holds both the planted truth (`spec`, `tacit`, `n_det`, `det_value`, `truth_decision`, `realize_outcome`, `arrivals_today`) and the run state (`ob`, `open_idx`, `next_id`) — `world.h:91-104`. Change: `Plant` keeps the truth and the generators; `Ledger` keeps `ob[]` and `open_idx` and is **built only by folding rows** (`ARRIVE` opens a cell, `ASSIGN` seats it, `CONTEXT` marks a system fetched, `DECIDE`/`EFFECT` closes it, `OUTCOME` settles it, `TICK` ages it). `human_day` and `world_arrive`/`world_settle` become Plant methods that append rows; the machine and the report read the Ledger. INTELLECT: the Ledger is folded from the CDC lane. Invariant: `Ledger == fold(tape)` at every period. **O14 · fold-to-identity**: rebuild the ledger cold from the tape after a run and `memcmp` it against the live one; the lie is a field set on a struct without a row.

**S2 · Put the missing fields on the tape.** `R_ARRIVE` must carry `day_due` (in `a`) and `dep` (in `b`) — today both are zero (`world.h:193`); `R_EFFECT` must carry `via` (in `flags`, beside `reversible`) — today `via` exists only on the struct (`world.h:86`) and the ladder reads it from there (`machine.h:586`). Add two fields to `Rec` without breaking the 36-byte width: `pad` becomes `firm` (a tenant id, S13) and a 16-bit `rev` replaces nothing yet needed — or widen to 40 bytes and re-pin the static_assert. The fold law then holds for the ladder: a cold fold reproduces `n_machine` and `n_assisted` exactly. Three smaller row-hygiene items ride along: `Rec::arm` must mean the *decider* (today `run_machine` stamps `arm = 1` on the humans' rows too, `main.cpp:159-160`, and the decider is really `seat == -1`); `R_OUTCOME.seat` must name who decided (today it is `o.seat`, which `Integrator::commit` never sets, `solver.h:333`); and every multiverse patch must land as an `R_PATCH` row carrying its inverse (today the writ and the planted spec are edited in place, `main.cpp:383, 388-390`, and `R_PATCH` is never emitted). **O15 · the ladder is a fold**: recompute every `Lic` from rows and compare.

**S3 · The judge port: `observe()` becomes `Judge::read(Frame)`.**
Today the machine's proposal is the plant's read physics with the machine's mask (`machine.h:450`), which is right for a sim and impossible for a product. Change: `struct Frame { cls; oid; the instrumented determinant values the machine can see; the class's precedent stats; }` produced by `serialize(ledger, oid, template)`; `struct Proposal { choice; direction; completeness_hat; judge_hash; }` produced by a `Judge`. Two judges ship: `PlantJudge` (sim: `observe` over the Frame's determinant values with the learned `mach_skill`, exactly today's arithmetic) and, in INTELLECT, the real ones — the compiled routine, the event model, the frontier call behind the provider seam (S16). Every `R_EFFECT` and `R_HOLD` row carries `judge_hash`; the ladder is per (class, band, judge). Invariant: the machine never calls `observe` on a `Plant`. **O16 · the judge is swappable**: run `--automate` with `PlantJudge` and with a constant-choice null judge; the null must license nothing (the e-process must not clear) — the lie is a judge that reads `truth_decision`.

**S4 · No planted truth on the machine's path.** Five reads to replace with folds: `FT_DFRAC` (`solver.h:103`) → `C.decide_frac[c]`, the compile step's measured value; `would_cost` and the review/sign costs (`machine.h:480-481, 520, 531`) → the metered minutes per class from the compile step and `C.n_systems[c]`, not `sp.n_systems`; `completeness_from` (`machine.h:422`) → the Frame's own coverage estimate (`C.coverage[c]` × the fraction of the join graph actually present in this cell's rows); n₀ (`core.h:324` via `machine.h:178, 362`) → the measured base rate, a fold over `R_OUTCOME`; `unlicensable` and `canary_rate` (`machine.h:359, 381`) → arrivals per term as a fold over `R_ARRIVE`. Today the resident consumes exactly one field of `Compiled`, `join_graph`; the other measured fields are printed and never used. And split `ClassSpec`: an authored **`Schema`** (name, wire, verdict_latency, value, reversible, warrant, arrival_per_day, base_rate — everything a person writes in ten lines) and a plant-only **`Planted`** (`decide_frac`, `n_systems`'s truth, tacit mass) held by `Plant`, not by `cls_spec()`. Invariant: `machine.h`, `solver.h`, `report.h` compile with `-DACME_NO_PLANT` where `world.h`'s planted section and `Planted` are `#error`. **O17 · the machine links without the plant**: a build target, not a runtime check; the lie is a `#include` that reaches `truth_decision`.

**S5 · The compile step's minute meter goes on the tape.** `decide_frac` is "measured" from `HumanStats::by_class` (`main.cpp:140`), an in-memory meter no real firm has. Either (a) the Plant appends an `R_ACT` row per human act with `minutes` in `value` and the act kind in `a` — the interior instrumented *only to be priced*, which `core.h:180` already permits for meetings — or (b) `decide_frac` is a rung-1 survey input (the runbook's time-use sample) written into `Schema`. Do (a) in sim so the fold is total, and admit (b) as the real-world source with its own `[survey]` provenance tag. **O13** already grades the recovered ranking; it now grades it from rows.

**S6 · The tape becomes the store.** Adopt fusord's tape wholesale (`fusord.cpp` §6): a durable append-only file, BLAKE2b-256 chain with the head recovered at open, torn-row recovery, a cursor committed as *ingested*, a checkpoint whose `.meta` carries the cursor. Keep the 36/40-byte fixed record and write it as binary segments (the whole point of `Rec` is that a fold is a scan, `core.h:161-162`), with a JSONL projection for the observer. `chaining=false` stays legal only for `--multiverse` forks and prints as such. Invariant: a restart resumes at the ingested cursor and the chain verifies from genesis. **O18 · the tape survives a kill**: kill the process mid-period, restart, verify the chain and fold-to-identity.

**S7 · The switch and the pill.** `<out>/intellect.state` ∈ {off, shadow, live, stop}, operator-writable only, read every period; `intellect.heartbeat.json` atomic every beat, staleness > 3 beats = STALLED (`fusord.cpp` §5). The gate takes the switch as an input: `off` → verdicts recorded, no `R_EFFECT` written, no effect request; `shadow` → `R_EFFECT` rows with `flags |= SHADOW`, drafts diffed against what the incumbent does on the same cell (the Resident spec's shadow mode, ≥80% draft agreement over K ≥ 5 before promotion); `live` → the valve. ACME defaults to `live` in the synthetic world because its world is the plant; INTELLECT defaults to `off`. **O19 · inert-off**: with the switch `off`, a run of the real-mode binary on a spool produces zero effect rows and a world byte-identical to the same spool with no machine (F-INERT's full form from Addendum F).

**S8 · The gate as a library.** Today `gate()` is a header function in `solver.h` beside the learned `Field` (`solver.h:291`). Move it to `gate.h` with no include but `core.h`, compile it as its own object with a pinned code hash, and stamp that hash on every verdict row (the resident organization §VI: "no learned component authored this decision" is then a fact a build verifies). Keep the published order. Invariant: the gate's object file has no symbol from `solver.h`, `machine.h` or `world.h`. **O2** already proves it never widens over the lattice; add the hash check to the header row.

**S9 · The retained stratum at every rung (F14's structural half).** The gate consults `in_canary` at rung 1 only (`solver.h:306`); at rungs ≥ 2 the flag relabels an act (`RS_CANARY`) and changes nothing, so `eps_floor` cannot bind and the human control arm goes to zero on licensed classes. Change: at every rung, an obligation drawn into the stratum is routed to the **incumbent arm** (the human, or in INTELLECT the seat that still exists) rather than acted — the control arm that keeps people practised and keeps the comparison alive (the resident organization §VII: "a sample never goes to zero"). Then `eps_floor` is the stratum's uniform floor and it binds by construction; the canary *rate* for licensing stays `min(cap, max(floor, n₀/V))`. Also lift the 0.35 cap into the writ so the receipt's finding — n₀/V > 0.35 in every class of this schema — becomes a printed schema fact rather than a silent clamp. **O20 · the stratum never reaches zero**: over a run, every licensed class-band shows incumbent decisions ≥ floor × arrivals; the lie is a rung-5 class with zero human rows.

**S10 · The salt, the seed and the license table leave the machine.** The salt (`firm.h:95`) moves out of `Writ` into `governor.salt`, a file the machine cannot read; the canary and audit draws are made by a `Governor` (in-process in sim, a separate process in INTELLECT — fusord's switch pattern generalized) and appended as an `R_STRATUM` row *before* the decision row; the gate reads the row. The frontier branch's act coin, drawn today from **the world's seed** (`u01(w.seed, 7200+c, oid)`, `machine.h:502`), becomes a property of the judge (a real rented mind either proposes or declines; in sim `PlantJudge` draws from its own key), never of the plant. The license table is written only by the governor (its `Ladder::step`) into `license.table`, read by the gate every period; the machine's process has no code path that writes it. Invariant: the machine's translation unit has no read of the salt or the world seed and no write of the table. **O21 · the salt is outside**: the machine's features carry zero mutual information with the draw (a planted feature-dependent draw is the lie).

**S10b · Retire the dead physics or make it live (F15).** `firm.h:274-310` defines `Decider`, `p_good` and `realize_outcome` as "the one dynamics source both arms call"; the audit finds **no caller anywhere**. The grader actually used is `truth_decision` — a sign test on the full weighted signal — plus `on_time`, inside `world_settle` (`world.h:223, 291-293`); the "completeness is the load-bearing variable" curve that `DESIGN.md` and the README describe is not what grades outcomes. This is an honest-instrument finding of the same kind as the seven in the first receipt: either `world_settle` calls `realize_outcome` (and the receipts are re-run, because every number changes) or the curve is deleted and the README's sentence with it. The brief takes no position on which; it takes the position that a documented physics with no caller is a defect. **O28 · the physics has a caller**: a build with `p_good` marked `[[deprecated]]` must warn, or the function is gone.

**S11 · Novelty on the Frame (F11).** `nov = novelty(c, |mach_complete − 0.7|)` (`machine.h:463`) is a per-class constant because the resident reads every instrumented system every time. Change: the conformal score runs over the Frame — the magnitude of the read signal against its class calibration in sim; the frame's embedding or the trunk's hidden state in INTELLECT. Now novelty has a tail and `RS_NOVEL` can fire on a case outside the population the license was earned on, which is the whole reason the third number exists. **O22 · novelty fires on a planted stranger**: plant obligations with determinant values drawn from a shifted distribution; the gate must route ≥ 90% of them to `V_WARRANT/RS_NOVEL`; the lie is the shift removed.

**S12 · The counsel memo (F12).** A `V_FRONTIER` cell that fails the rented mind's act draw is re-gated and re-rented daily (`machine.h:494-515`). Change: the frontier's proposal is a row (`R_COUNSEL`, carrying `judge_hash` and the Frame hash), memoized by (oid, frame hash); re-ask only when a newer row on the cell changes the frame. This is TAPESTRY's cognitive index applied to counsel and it is also the SCRIPTORIUM provider seam's cache discipline: the frozen template prefix is the byte-identical system message, the cell's rows are the volatile tail. **O23 · counsel is not re-rented on an unchanged frame**: identical frame twice → one call; the lie is a memo keyed on oid alone.

**S13 · Firm identity and tenancy.** Every row carries a `firm` id (the `pad` byte, or a 16-bit field on a 40-byte record); one tape per firm; `firm.json` is the schema map (S4's `Schema` + seats + writ) that `build_acme` generates in sim and a person plus the template compiler author in INTELLECT — the runbook's one hand-written object. N firms are N tapes and N license tables under one governor; χ becomes observable across tenants (the head theorem: the second client is the instrument). **O24 · two firms, one binary**: two synthetic tapes with different seeds run interleaved produce the same rows as two separate runs.

**S14 · The clock.** The period loop lives in main (`main.cpp:156-163`); `human_day` and `world_settle` take `day`. Change: the Plant appends `R_TICK` rows and the machine runs `period()` when it sees one — sim ticks daily, INTELLECT ticks on a configured period (15 minutes, an hour) with events between ticks ingested as they land. `run_machine` becomes the same loop over a spool that fusord runs over a lane: `drain_intake(); if tick: period();`. Invariant: the machine reads time only from `TICK` rows. **O25 · the world never dilates**: a spool with the same rows at different wall-clock spacing yields identical verdict rows.

**S15 · The fitted world.** Add `WorldParams` (arrival rates, verdict latencies, base rates, value per class, seat attention and specialty, the incumbent's decision distribution per class-band, dependency rate, slack) with two constructors: `Plant::synthesize(seed)` (today's `make_world`/`build_acme`) and `Plant::fit(tape, firm.json)` (estimated from a real tape). `--multiverse` then forks either. ## §7 · Kills for the transition design
